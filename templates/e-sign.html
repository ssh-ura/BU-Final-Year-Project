Terms of Business

This document sets out the basis on which Mortgage Hive Ltd will provide mortgage and protection advice to you, including the scope of our service, how we are regulated, and your right to cancel.

Mortgage Hive Ltd is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. We offer mortgages and protection products from a representative panel of lenders and insurers, and the recommendations we make to you are based on your stated needs, objectives, and circumstances as captured during the fact-find.

You will be advised by a qualified mortgage and protection adviser. Where a product is recommended, we will provide a written suitability report explaining why the product is appropriate for you and disclosing any features that may not be in your best interest.

You have the right to cancel any mortgage or protection contract within the statutory cooling-off period, which will be disclosed alongside the relevant product documentation. Any complaints can be raised in writing to our compliance team, and if unresolved, referred to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

Fee Agreement

This document explains how Mortgage Hive Ltd is paid for the advice we provide, including any fees payable by you and any commission we receive from lenders or insurers.

We charge a flat advice fee of £499, payable on submission of your mortgage application to the chosen lender. If your application is declined and we cannot place it with an alternative lender on materially similar terms, the fee is refundable in full.

In addition, we will receive a procuration fee from your chosen lender on completion of the mortgage. The amount varies by lender and product and will be disclosed to you in writing before you commit to the application. Where a protection product is recommended, the insurer pays us a commission, which will also be disclosed.

You may cancel this agreement at any time before submission of your application at no cost. Cancellation after submission may incur the advice fee if work has already been carried out on your behalf.
